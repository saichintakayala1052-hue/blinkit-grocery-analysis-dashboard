# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, outlet efficiency, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution. This project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using KPIs, DAX, Power Query, and interactive visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance across different outlet types, outlet sizes, locations, and product categories. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify sales trends, and make data-driven decisions through interactive reports.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

To conduct a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution using various KPIs and interactive visualizations in Power BI.

# 📌 Dashboard Insights

### 📊 Sales Performance
- Total revenue exceeded **$1.20 Million**.
- Average sales per transaction are **140.99**.
- More than **9,000 items** were sold.
- Customer satisfaction remains high with an average rating of **3.92**.

### 🏪 Outlet Performance
- Medium-sized outlets contribute the highest sales.
- Tier 3 locations generate the maximum revenue.
- Supermarket Type 1 is the best-performing outlet category.

### 📦 Product Analysis
Top-performing categories include:
- Fruits & Vegetables
- Snack Foods
- Household
- Frozen Foods

Lower-performing categories include:
- Seafood
- Breakfast
- Others

### 📅 Sales Trend
- Outlets established around **2018** generated the highest sales.
- Sales remained relatively stable across other years.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for high-selling products.
- Expand medium-sized outlets in Tier 3 cities.
- Improve the performance of low-selling product categories.
- Focus marketing campaigns on top-performing outlet locations.
- Maintain high customer satisfaction through product quality improvements.
